How it works

Three commands. The CLI is one static binary; the tapes live on our hosted service or your own S3 bucket.

  1. 01

    Wrap your test command

    Prefix the CI step with tapedeck record --. Works with Jest, pytest, Go test, RSpec and Gradle. Overhead in our benchmarks: 3-7%.

  2. 02

    Keep only the red runs

    Green runs are discarded after the job. Failed and retried-then-passed runs are uploaded, so you also catch the flakes your retry policy hides.

  3. 03

    Replay and attach a debugger

    tapedeck replay feeds the recorded clock, randomness and network back in. Set a breakpoint in Delve, pdb or your IDE and run it as many times as you need.
