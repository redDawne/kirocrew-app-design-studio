# Month-in-review brief

**Idea:** Editorial take for the finance lead who wants the story before the rows: one large MRR figure with a written sentence on what moved it, a single bar explaining the net change, then short action and renewal lists and the largest accounts; assumes a monthly close cadence.

**Critic notes:**

- R5/R1: the stacked bar summed gains and losses into one 'gross movement' strip, which misreads as all positive; replaced with Gained vs Lost bars on one scale (+$60,325 / -$20,598) so the $39,727 net is legible at a glance.
- R1: lead sentence left an orphan "it." under the big number; widened the copy column to 6/12, rewrote the sentence with a firmer point of view.
- R3: tightened top padding (py-12 to py-8, hero gap 48 to 40) so the accounts table fits above the fold-and-a-half instead of being cut.
- R4: avatar sat alone at the bottom of the icon rail, off-screen at desktop height; moved it to the header next to the period picker.
- R12: lists trimmed to 4 items each (links say 'All 7') so the accounts table fits the 1400px page.
