# Streak as headline

**Idea:** An editorial screen that leads with one big serif streak number and a sentence about it, then a bar-strip of the week and a plain list of what is left, because the streak is what keeps people coming back; assumes the app picks the user's longest live streak to feature.

**Critic notes:**
